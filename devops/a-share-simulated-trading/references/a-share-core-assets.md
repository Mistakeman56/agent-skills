# A-Share Core Assets (白马股/核心资产)

## Overview

Stable-growth A-share stocks with consistent profitability, high ROE, and defensive characteristics. These serve as "底仓" (foundation positions) in a portfolio — they won't spike like concept stocks but also won't crash as hard.

Use these when the user asks about "稳定增长的股" or needs a defensive allocation alongside speculative positions.

## Key Differences from Concept Stocks

| Dimension | Core Assets | Xiaomi Concept Stocks |
|-----------|-------------|----------------------|
| Volatility | Low (1-3% daily) | High (3-10% daily) |
| Holding period | Months to years | Days to weeks |
| Growth rate | 10-20% CAGR | Event-driven, unpredictable |
| Dividend | Often pays dividends | Rarely pays |
| Risk of total loss | Very low | Moderate (概念 fade) |
| Best for | Portfolio ballast | Active trading |
