## Key Risks

- **Cause ≠ Effect:** Xiaomi's stock price and A-share concept stocks are correlated but the correlation is loose. A-share concept stocks may not track Xiaomi 1:1.
- **Concept fade:** Concepts rotate quickly in A-shares. A stock can drop out of "hot concept" status even if Xiaomi does well.
- **Individual stock risk:** Each supplier has its own business risks beyond Xiaomi relationship.
- **Market cap matters:** Prefer ¥100亿+ market cap stocks for stability.
