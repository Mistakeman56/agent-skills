# Hong Kong Stock Trading Guide for Mainland Users

## Overview

When users ask about buying Hong Kong stocks (e.g., Xiaomi 01810, Tencent 00700, Meituan 03690), they need practical routes as a mainland Chinese resident. This guide covers the current (2026) regulatory landscape.

## Current Routes (2026)

### ❌ Route 1: 港股通 (Stock Connect)
- Requires A-share account with **日均 50万 RMB** assets for 20 consecutive trading days
- Not viable for students or those without significant savings
- **Pass for most users**

### ❌ Route 2: Futu/Tiger Brokers (富途/老虎)
- **COMPLETELY CLOSED** for new mainland residents as of September 2025
- Now requires海外永居身份 (overseas permanent residency)
- Source: yicai.com, September 22, 2025 report
- **Pass for all mainland users without海外身份**

### ✅ Route 3: Hong Kong Bank Account (Recommended)
Still feasible in 2026, but **no longer "zero-threshold"**:

| Bank | Requirements | Notes |
|------|-------------|-------|
| 招商永隆 | App预开户+到港柜台, 无存款门槛 | **Most friendly**, ~5min at counter |
| 汇丰 HSBC | 近3月日均≥1万HKD, 否则$100HKD/月管理费 | Stricter since 2026 |
| 中银香港 | 需预约到港柜台 | Requires appointment |
| 信银国际 | 线上开户已关闭, 需柜台+工资流水/在职证明 | **Most strict** since 2025 |

**Essential documents:**
- 港澳通行证 (有效期 ≥ 6个月, 有有效签注)
- 内地身份证
- 开通国际漫游的手机号 (收短信)
- 3个月内地址证明 (可选,部分银行要求)
- 常用内地银行卡

### ✅ Route 4: Brokers Still Accepting Mainland (Requires HK Bank Card)

| Broker | Notes |
|--------|-------|
| 盈立证券 (Yingli) | 最友好的中文App, 但入金需要香港银行卡 |
| 盈透证券 (IB) | 老牌稳定, 全英文, 需境外账户 |
| 卓锐 (Zhuorui) | 小众, 能开 |

**Key catch:** ALL brokers require a Hong Kong bank account for 入金 (deposit). Getting the HK bank card is the bottleneck.

### Route 5: US Brokers (If user has US bank account)
- Charles Schwab, Firstrade — accept mainland users
- Need USD/HKD deposited from a foreign bank account
- Not practical for students

## The Real Bottleneck: 外汇管制 (Forex Controls)

- Mainland China: **$50,000 USD/year** per person forex quota
- Converting RMB → HKD and getting it to a HK account is the hardest step
- Common paths:
  - 银联卡 direct withdraw from HK ATM (daily limit ~8K HKD, small amounts)
  - Wire transfer from mainland bank to HK bank (needs supporting docs)
  - 熊猫速汇 / Wise — some work, some don't for mainland users

## Practical Recommendations

### For students / low-income users:

1. **Short-term:** Can't realistically trade HK stocks without visiting Hong Kong
2. **Plan:** Next trip to HK → 招商永隆 App预开户 → counter 5min → done
3. **Minimal amount:** Deposit ~10,000 HKD to avoid monthly fees
4. **Buy Xiaomi/others:** Via bank's own trading app after account is set up

### When user asks "怎么买小米股票":

1. First explain HK stocks are separate from A-share market
2. Ask if they can visit HK (tourism), or know someone who can
3. If YES → Recommend 招商永隆 route
4. If NO → Recommend waiting or use the simulated trading to practice
